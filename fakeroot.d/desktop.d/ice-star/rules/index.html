Ice Star — Rules

Bannable Offenses

Breaking the following rules will result in a ban:

  • No cheating. This includes the Sonic MOD jump system, spindash, dropped ring collection. However, Tails' flying system from the Advanced Client mod is allowed.
  • No exploiting or hacking. This includes chat spamming, rejoining to avoid votekick, entity art scripts, and ability delay mods. Custom palettes are allowed.
A rule violation is still a violation — even in retaliation. If another player is breaking the rules, ask an admin for help, leave the server, switch lobbies, or start a votekick. Do not take matters into your own hands.

Soft Rules & Recommendations

These are not strictly enforced, but please be mindful:

  • Don't camp bodies or constantly target the same player. It ruins the experience for others. You likely won't be banned, but you may be kicked by an admin or voted out.
  • Don't harass anyone in chat. There is no /mute command, so persistent harassment will result in a kick.
Use common sense and be respectful. If unsure about something, ask an admin or other players.
